🐾 Nomadic Animals

Find one. Scan it. Take it somewhere new.

Every Animal Has a Story

Nomadic Animals are small traveling animals released into the world. Each one carries a unique QR code that lets you discover where it has been and add the next chapter to its journey.

If you found one, congratulations — you're now part of its adventure.

How It Works

🐶

1. Find an Animal

Discover a Nomadic Animal somewhere along its journey.

📱

2. Scan the QR Code

Scan its QR code to see where the animal has traveled.

🌎

3. Keep It Moving

Take it somewhere new, record the location, and release it for the next person to find.

How Far Can They Travel?

Across towns, across states, or even across the world — every person who finds a Nomadic Animal helps build its travel story. Follow along and see where the adventure leads.
